The gap

A closed window is not a closed app.

On macOS the red button hides the window and leaves the app running — in your Dock, in your memory, in your Cmd-Tab. People arriving from Windows expect otherwise, and after twenty years so do plenty of Mac users.

macOS on its own

You close the last window ↓ The window disappears. The app keeps running.

With RedButtonQuit

You close the last window ↓ The app quits, and the moment is written down.

How it decides

Three things happen between your click and the quit.

It watches

RedButtonQuit uses the macOS Accessibility API to see window events across your regular apps. That is the only way macOS lets an app know a window closed.

It waits

Entering or leaving fullscreen destroys a window and builds a new one. So it pauses one second first. If a real window appears, the quit is called off.

It confirms

The app is asked to quit the normal way, so unsaved work still stops it. Then RedButtonQuit checks whether the app really exited, and records what it found.

The record

Proof that it worked.

A utility that works silently is indistinguishable from a utility that broke silently. RedButtonQuit keeps a plain list of the apps it closed and when — and of the times it decided not to, so a surprise is never a mystery.

  • ▪Only its own work. Apps you quit yourself never appear.
  • ▪Confirmed, not assumed. An entry says "quit" only after the app actually exited.
  • ▪Yours alone. Kept on your Mac, capped, and clearable in one click.

What it is, exactly

RequiresmacOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple silicon or Intel.
PermissionAccessibility. It is the only macOS API that reports window events, and the app cannot work without it.
Network useNone. The app contains no networking code. It never checks in, phones home, or updates itself.
Data collectedNone. No analytics, no crash reporting, no account. The quit history stays in one file on your Mac.
Protected appsFinder, Dock, System Settings and other system apps are never quit. You can exclude any other app yourself.
SignatureDeveloper ID certificate issued to INITIATOR LLC, notarized by Apple, ticket stapled.
LicenceMIT. Every line is on GitHub, and you can build it yourself.
PriceFree. There is no paid tier and no upsell.
